# Chronicler’s notes
Chronicler: Ilick

After we woke up Tip seemed to wander off somewhere, but we found ourselves rejoined by Hollb Puffhelm, who appeared just in time for us to conjure more of our Pook Morels, having a respectable group of five. We proceeded in the direction Ygraine gave us to Bumble’s manor, knowing that Hollb in particular would want to meet her what with her goal of getting to the moon.

We entered yet another boggy, uneven woodland with a ripe cheese smell where we discovered curiously large mycelial tendrils much like that of the Myconom, only they were orange and appeared to be both oozing a cheesy substance and growing little waxy red buds. Some sampling determined that it indeed seemed to be secreting a kind of brie and growing gouda like fruiting bodies, which was absolutely fascinating from our perspective. Was this a fungus that had evolved or been tampered with to produce dairy like qualities, or had a dairy product been altered by some magic to become fungal? Either way it reinforced our own belief that everything was and still is, ultimately, fungal!

We followed the tendrils to their source, where we came upon a large, living manor supporting a giant orange puffball mushroom, with trellises established to harvest all sorts of differing cheeses. There were also a few Mosslings tending to the fields, though we noticed no rat folk as the rumour mentioned. Smodri immediately hounded one of the workers for more local rumours, hearing about a lost shrine of St. Joriel, while the others explained what was happening: the cheese was being made to be sold and provide various helpful effects, much like a potion or tonic or good herb, Bumble wanting to create some of the items she might have needed in her heyday of adventuring.

Hollb, needless to say, was beside themselves, eager to try an ashy, purple cheese that would provide fire resistance. Kollb was happy to provide samples, but as the cheeses were quite expensive and limited due to needing to be aged properly, wasn’t about to just let some of it go.

We were more interested in speaking with Bumble, so we approached the manor, being let in by another Mossling butler, Bumble seeming to have a whole proper staff here. We aren’t usually one for buildings or settlements, but the living mycotecture of Mumblebowl Manor was something we could definitely appreciate…although poor Nannigrew had to keep ducking and stooping to fit inside the Mossling sized construction and was very self conscious about her horns damaging any of the fleshy, spongey walls.

Bumble was in her library and a very advanced Mossling by appearance. She was practically perfectly spherical, with yellow mould hair, bracket fungi ears, and toadstools sprouting from her eyes. She seemed very personable and was eager to tell us all about her ambition to go to the moon after having discovered a ratfolk diary that detailed their own journey from the moon, as well as the schematics for making such a journey, having apparently travelled here and settled somewhere near Cobton on the Shiver fifty or sixty years or so ago. Her current plan seemed to be using the entire manor as a craft which would work as a sort of balloon or dirigible that just needed a bit more fuel to be able to make the complete journey, other attempts involving an amulet of density alteration. 

Impressed by her ability to grow something so large and multifunctional, though perhaps slightly disappointed it wasn’t as sapient and potent as the Myconom, we asked her more about her adventures, especially as she mentioned Draxus Jerricorn helping her with translations. She had apparently ventured with two others, a thief by the name of Bweydot of Smerm that the others had heard rumours of haunting a road in the north of the woods and a Cleric. She also explained a bit more of the other cheeses she had on offer: Creamhorn, which would shrink you to the size of a rat, and Lichenyarg, which was a rare healing substance with no negative side effects. Knowing we were adventurers ourselves, she seemed keen on selling us some…which was perhaps what motivated poor Moggét to do what he did…

Apparently while we’d all been having this conversation, he had co-ordinated with Conchwin to try and create illusory gold to con Bumble out of some of the valuable cheese…which traumatised one Mossling worker and caused Kollb to immediately storm into the mansion to get help, not stopping even as Moggét tried to smooth things over, and then retreated swiftly with Conchwin into the forest before he could face any consequences.

Needless to say, Bumble was not happy with ‘Fairy Trickery’, helping out her workers and complaining about the company we were keeping. Nannigrew helped distract her ire by mentioning our other encounters with Fairy and Hollb seemed starstruck with Bumble in general. This left Smodri and ourselves on our own in the library, where he discovered a trapdoor that he sensed a room full of fermenting cheeses and iron chests behind and we discovered a book Nannigrew might be partial to in liturgic entitled “Diuretics of our Deity”. This transpired to be a book of recipes intent on cleaning the sin out of individuals, perhaps quite literally given the title, but Nannigrew did seem pleased to receive it.  
  
_Vessel Moggét flees with our stolen Snail…encounters a bearded male Vessel…is accosted by thrown mandrakes…oh yes, it begins, it begins indeed…Vessel Moggét recovers a jar with summer scenes…opens it to create a beacon of sunny weather, pinpointing his location…  
  
_We tracked down Moggét easily enough from the sunbeams pouring down on him, deciding to make camp in the woods after searching for anything else interesting, not wanting to disturb Bumble further. Smodri and ourselves came across a number of useful looking herbs and fungi, including some cream coloured floppy varietals, some black meddlers, and some ramble frond fern. Nannigrew cooked for us all, making sure to give Moggét and Conchwin a meal from the book to help cleanse their sin.

This may or may not have lead to them being somewhat distracted while taking their watch as we slept…  
  
_Vessel Moggét touched by silken strands…Vessel Moggét wraps themselves up in webbing…Vessel Moggét refuses to call for help or wake anyone but Conchwin…creating illusions to try and escape…only rousing some other Vessels from struggle against the Giant Spiders…Vessel Nannigrew sets one on fire…Vessel Hollb gets wrapped…Vessel Moggét bitten, poisoned, deeply!  
  
_…We awoke to a scene of mild chaos, a trio of giant spiders having attacked the group. We swiftly put two of them asleep and unravelled the web threads from Hollb with our whistling as Smodri finished off the fleeing, flaming spider, but it seemed Moggét was very badly poisoned.  
  
We went as quickly as we could back to Mumblebowl manor, Bumble not keen on helping Moggét, but begrudgingly giving him some of the Lichenyarg to delay the poison’s effect and telling us where to find an herb that might help: Wallowmoss.  
  
Exhausted and needing rest, but desperate to save Moggét, we first traveled through an area of phallic brown fungi that appeared to be broadcasting some sort of chant. Smodri blocked it with a countercharm song, but Hollb wished to risk and see what they were saying, the chants resolving into a continuous, “The Myconom Desires You, Westward Lies the Font.” Upon hearing this, we assured the fungi we would be approaching the Myconom soon once we found a suitable offering, we just had to save our friend first. This seemed to please them, which bodes well for our eventual meeting!  
  
We then entered an area of rubbery black antler like fungi dripping orange sap like substances that were extremely sticky. We questioned our platoon of Pook Morels as to its nature, as they told us it was a very useful substance for gagging and silencing people and could only really be removed with alcohol or some other potent substance. We then, as a group, foraged every inch of the place, knowing Moggét had precious little time left. Smodri found some Mossmulch fungus and we managed to find a couple precious sprigs of fluffy yellow buds of Wallowmoss!

Carefully placing a bud in Moggét’s maw and helping him chew, it soon transpired that it didn’t appear to have any effect on his poisoning, and not even repeat doses appeared to help. So we slung him onto Conchwin and had the giant snail speed towards Blackswell, in the hopes the apothecary there would have the one other only known cure for poisoning in the woods.  
  
_Vessel Moggét tries to reach settlement…encounters two bears…Sacrifice Conchwin uses psionic to distract and continue…little time left…arrive…no cure available…no hope…Vessel Moggét enters apothecary…applies flame…perishes. …as we promise…  
  
_We arrive at Blackswell to the smell and sight of smoke, and fear perhaps the worst has occurred…

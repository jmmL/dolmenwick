# Chronicler’s notes
Chronicler: Ilick

We spent some time with Creakeek, the Nutcap scientist, discussing his elixir of life in greater detail. We perhaps may have lost track of time, as Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water and Samwise departed without us and we didn’t notice, focused on applying principles of mycology to the elixir to allow for greater communication and connection from the subjects.

_Vessel Nannigrew takes custody of Vessel Odell…Vessel Mogget rejoins…they travel, approaching us…_

It seems Odell is travelling with Nannigrew after recovering from his injuries, as well as another we haven’t met before named Mogget Flippancy! He appears to be a Grimalkin of a similarly chaotic, yet more enchanting nature than Tip. They came upon us in Creakeek’s study and seemed somewhat disturbed by the talk of the Elixir, Odell looking like he might be stirred to violence, but we think we explained it all rationally enough that they are completely fine with it now! Nannigrew even attempted to communicate with some of the transfigured individuals, the plants shaking in rudimentary gesture.

Creakeek let us stay the night in his home and Nannigrew wished to take some of the plants out, presumably to help reverse their extreme modifications, which the Nutcap was less than pleased about, until we offered to take just one as an ‘experiment’ to see how the outside world would affect it. Naming it ‘Bill’ we departed in search of the Myconom. Or at least, that was our specific goal, we suspect the others were much more interested in the treasure map Draxus Jerricorn provided. Odell even commented on how it was chivalrous to follow such maps and Mogget was clearly interested in the material wealth…we did our best to encourage such expectations.

It was around elevenses when we discovered a dell within a forest of woody toadstools and finger shaped fungi, a number of rotting purple mushrooms dotting the entrance of a well hidden cave mouth. Inside a trail of glowing pink toadstools led to a large red statue of some sort of slug like being with various rare fungal offerings left beneath it. According to Nannigrew it was likely a depiction of the Mossling deity Umbombra. We left one our rare Mottlecaps at its base.

It was at this time that a Mossling woman emerged, initially wanting to chase the other three out of her cave, but then saw us and we began conversing more rationally. She had maroon eyes, white mycelial hair, a giant puffball as a hat or possibly merged like our own situation, and was a mold oracle. She went by the name of Gropoll. We naturally had many things to discuss, largely in Mulch, while she kept studying and poking our companions, much to Odell’s chagrin. 

Crucially, she knew of the Myconom and even gave advice as to what it would enjoy as an offering, Dream Snail meat! Especially salami. She said Dream Snails were all over, but could be tricky to approach, especially if sleeping, apparently some sort of reality bleeding effect happening if they were dreaming. We will have to see if we can find some. She also seemed to believe the Myconom was merely an aspect of Umbombra. We didn’t have the heart to correct her that it was likely the other way around, but then, it is always so wonderfully difficult to tell with fungi…they always surprise you!

She seemed more than willing to let us stay and visit, but clearly had exhausted her patience with the other three, the drawbacks to a sedentary, hermetic life, we suppose, it’s partly why we decided to travel and adventure with others. As we kept travelling we came upon a forest with trees covered in soft white fungi and a much more difficult to traverse, bog like terrain. We also began to sense another Ley Line, this one producing the sensation of moaning and the dead, which Nannigrew pointed out was one Bogfrink had felt before, thus we must be on the same line. We could pinpoint it on the map now, provided it ran straight between the two points. We suppose it would only make sense Draxus would bury his treasure on a Ley Line!

That was not the only oddity we noticed, however. There were many strange bloody tentacle growths that we had actually heard rumour of with oddly specific shaped bulges within them humanoid and animal. Mogget curiously sliced one open, yet inside wasn’t the remains of something being digested, but what looked more like a series of mismatched or misgrown bones and organs. They also appeared to get more and more plentiful the closer we got to the pool of water Draxus had sunk the treasure in, which was a deep opaque black rather than clear as he described. It was unclear whether what he had buried had caused the contamination and the bloody tentacles or something else entirely.

Regardless, we all got to work finding and fishing out the treasure, using our thread whistling to first discover and then anchor a rope to the embedded object, then having Odell hoist it out with the encouragement of Nannigrew. It appeared to be some sort of cube like shape made of a curious metallic alloy. Though before we could examine it closely, we heard our own voices calling out mockingly, repeating back the words we had said earlier. Mogget grabbed the treasure and turned invisible, intent on whatever was stalking us not getting its hands on it, while the rest of us prepared to flee…though Odell, guided by his ideals of being a knight and perhaps his inexperience of the woods and such beings as these, said he would be able to protect us from the danger.  
  
Emerging from the southwest was a massive, ten foot tall creature, lithe and deer like with shaggy white fur and seven tails. It had a single, massive red eye and a fanged mouth that appeared to be frothing rather violently. Even Odell realised he was perhaps out of his league here and fled with us and Nannigrew, though the beast managed to catch up to him. It attempted to do something horrifying with its gaze, but failed. We suspect it may be what was behind all the bloody piles of tentacles…

Odell fought as best he could, though he quickly realised he wasn’t going to make any headway against the creature, doing all he could just to survive and let Nannigrew get away, who had also been attacked trying to drag him along. We attempted to use our newly made spell, figuring it could work as a distraction and even a point of attack at best, or some healing or other small benefits as we fled at worst. Fortunately, while it caused no harm, it did not in fact create anything useful.

Odell fled in a different direction so we could split the beast’s attention, with Nannigrew and us heading northwards, emerging at the large dark granite pylon Draxus mentioned in his map. We decided it would be a good rendezvous point for both Mogget and Odell if he managed to survive and escape the beast. As we discussed our spellcraft with Nannigrew, Odell emerged, heavily scarred, but alive, with Mogget joining us in time to seek camp for the night.

Examining the monolith to see if it would be a safe location, Nannigrew recognised it as being tended to, with thorns and blackberries carefully curated around it, also recognising an eye like relief in the top of it as a dedication to the Wood God Hasturia. Usually depicted as a skeleton of plasma and flame with a single flashing eye, the god of fire and embers, and also most commonly followed by witches and used in witchcraft. Such a malevolent presence was not seen as useful to rest under, so we moved deeper into the woods.

As we foraged for food, we came across some Black Clover, which fortuitously allows one to communicate with inanimate objects. Mogget immediately set about using it to communicate with the puzzle box of Draxus that we recovered, charming it into opening and revealing a large hoard of gems, a cloak and three spell books, which we were eager to peruse.

The next morning we travelled onwards, moving through sodden ground, twisted trees and a thick mist, the Ley Line still very much being present to our senses. We came upon a series of barrows and crypts, deciding to cautiously explore one with the blessing of Nannigrew, moving past the rotting wooden door to discover a clear pool of water under a small empty font with every other path filled with rubble. Nannigrew examined the frescoes around the crypt, noting they were of a language entirely foreign to the Wood, and Mogget decided to experiment with the font, filling it with water from the pool. Once it filled, the water appeared to change within the font, turning a rather recognisable pink in hue, much like the nymph pool we bathed in before…though it also triggered some form of gas trap! Thankfully it seemed to disperse relatively quickly and was easy enough to block, the healing water indeed seeming to help all three of the others, even Bill, the plant appreciating being watered with it. A shame we couldn’t bottle it as it seems the water turns back to normal the moment it leaves the font.

The place did provide a useful spot to rest for the night, as travelling and exploration was too slow to proceed much further, so we did and then emerged the next day on the eight of Haelholm, travelling further east…this time coming across recognisably fungal dominated woods, pallid toadstools, rotting trees, even rabbits with toadstools growing from their ears. The air was heavy with spores to the point of visibly being dispersed from the large toadstools at rhythmic intervals. While we were reasonably certain that we would be fine, for the safety of our companions and to give them the suitable respect for dealing with mycological beings, we advised covering their mouths and noses with some form of barrier, if only to prevent another incident like with poor Bredhr.

As we explored the area we eventually came across a large white tendril, clearly a huge mycelial strand, laying across the path. We placed our hand on it, seeing a vision of many of these strands converging in a nearby cave. The others seemed a little unnerved when we reported this vision, perhaps we looked a touch alarming when we were communing? We were uncertain as to whether it would be fruitful approaching the Myconom without an offering of Dream Snail meat, but the others brought up the fact that it would be useful to pinpoint its location at least.

We followed the tendril to its point of origin, hearing a low song coming from a massive white and blue mound, seeing several Mosslings singing in Mulch, tending to absolutely the most colossal fruiting body of any fungus we have ever seen or even dared imagine, each with a green glowing mushroom pulsing in their heads. Naturally, this rather unnerved our companions, given their synchronised movements and clear signs of being colonised, but we were not afraid of such natural processes, knowing this could be a wonderful opportunity for us. We almost rushed forward to join them…but our rational minds won out. We needed a suitable offering. After all, the Myconom would have no reason to form the kind of mutualistic relationship with us we wished if we had nothing to provide it beyond simple admiration.

Thankfully we discovered something else that was more to the tastes of Nannigrew and Odell in particular…a forgotten shrine within the Myconom’s own territory, covered in moss and lichen. Apparently it is the shrine of St. Cornice, Patron Saint of Chastity, Beetles and Parlour Maids. Honestly, the story sounded a little derivative and vaguely sexist, but perhaps we are a bit biased. The pair even recovered a holy relic, the Apron of St. Cornice herself.

Thus successful in our explorations and objectives, we decided the most sensible course of action would be to head to the nearby town of Blackeswell, where we could perhaps find a hunter to track down some Dream Snails and make it easier for other members of our group to find us. A place of dismal grey trees, white toadstools, run down buildings and a large green pond, named the Blacke for some reason, we came across a familiar group of woodgrue! The last we saw of them was back near Lankshorn by the Woodgrue Ears, though Mogget mentioned seeing them on a booze cruise, and they apparently have been having continued adventures, covered head to toe in orange slime. We joined them in the Spigot & Gullet to celebrate and swap tales, drinking far too much to find anyone of use that evening, but the following day Mogget managed to procure the services of a Grimalkin Hunter known as Penny Pouncemouse! Which felt oddly familiar for some reason we cannot pinpoint…

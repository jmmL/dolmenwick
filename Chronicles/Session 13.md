# Chronicler’s notes
Chronicler: Ilick

Struck with inspiration for a new arcane spell and with Mogget and Nannigrew and Odell not feeling particularly urgent in their need to go elsewhere, plus secure in the knowledge that the Myconom would likely not just suddenly be moving elsewhere, we decided to spend a good period of time in Blackeswell.

*Vessel Smodri learn of the Doerling vessels. Possible value in magical blades. Vessel Nannigrew pursue intrigue. Land ownership. Mines for hag iron. Vessel Klep. Vessel Mogget incapacitated with alcoholic substance. Attempted reproduction with a human construction. New Mossling Vessel. Vessel Hollb Puffhelm. Also arcane. Ideal for-*

We completed our spell quite readily, and though it would cost a bit to continually use, we believe the cost will be well worth it for the network of support we shall now have at our fingertips! We were also surprised to see so many of our old companions in Blackeswell, but we suppose they must have heard we had come out here. Interestingly, Smodri revealed that Dream Snails were partial to magical mosses and fungi and attracted to devouring the arcane, which seemed rather conveniently pointed at us…but then, taking a wider perspective, it would make sense the Myconom would detest such creatures as well, as it is very much on the menu, not that we believe any Dream Snail could make a dent in such an entrenched mycelium! They would perhaps hinder its expansion efforts though.

It was the second of Reedwryme and getting frosty. There was news of Lord Ramius approaching Castle Brackenwald regarding the capture of a beast, which we’re fairly certain would have been of interest to Odell, possibly explaining where he disappeared off to. Lady Harrowmore was also apparently authorising research up to the north, and the sorceress Igraine was sending a delegation to Prigwort as well. Lord Malbleat, meanwhile, seems keen to claim Whitefalls as his legitimate domain, we suspect there might be some sort of arcane or political secret there he wants to seize.

Regardless of how the wood was moving around us, we decided to press further north and northeast on the hunt of a Dream Snail, also keen to explore the farthest corner of the wood. It was a very pleasant area, with pungent soft soil, piles of mottled leaves and swollen fungi. Penny the hunter was leading us around, finding a small hamlet that we approached curiously, noticing a bunch of inhabitants that appeared to be shambling and undead even from a distance. Smodri’s woodkenning came up with despair and the name “Ayswell Dregger”. Both ourselves and Hollb noticed we were very much still on the same Ley Line we had been feeling earlier, giving the feeling of despair.

Tip snuck in to get a closer look at the situation, finding himself in a large distillery of sorts where they manufactured some sort of whiskey from the peat of the boggy terrain itself. Realising the inhabitants were probably sapient and friendly enough to at least approach (and also after helping themselves to a flask of the whisky) we entered the town. The undead inhabitants seemed curious and wanting to communicate, but it became apparent that either their vocal cords had deteriorated and rendered them incapable of speech, or it was just a by product of their reanimated nature. We managed to procure some writing implements and found some more literate inhabitants who conveyed that for the most part people just seemed to be dying nearby, being reanimated and finding the town on their own, though they didn’t know much more than that.   
  
We entered the distillery through the front, where a huge reanimated woman by the name of Bertha Bogborn was able to speak to us more directly, apparently the whiskey she was distilling enabled them to talk, as well as provided all the other more usual effects a good whiskey would normally have. Mogget, Shade-of-Winter’s-Betrayal, and honestly quite a large portion of the group seemed to engage in very elaborate plans and behaviours to try and swindle Bertha out of a good portion of the whiskey using glamours and false treasure and quick talking, which we felt was somewhat counterproductive, especially when Bertha managed to catch Mogget even when invisible and give him a few smacks against the counter, souring relations a fair amount. Still, the more of them we have between us and the Dream Snail’s appetite, the better. And it did make for a somewhat amusing scene to watch safely from the doorway.

The newcomer, Hollb, seemed to have the knack for communicating with birds, which became rather interesting when it transpired the blackbirds he was attempting to communicate with were actually also undead and could not communicate until given a bit of the purloined whiskey. They spoke of something called an “Endstone” …which after careful exploring, we seemed to discover, hidden very well in a copse of trees. A fifteen foot talk green grey rock monolith with veins of red running through it, clearly shaped, but also much more naturally forming and following veins of existing ore than say the witches’ monolith we discovered just outside Draxus’ hiding place. It was being attended by a group of Drune who appeared both to be conducting some sort of lecture or learning around it, as well as also being undead too from the way they were pale and the lecturer having weapons embedded in several vital areas. Curiously they also seemed to be fully aware of the town and the people in it given the few words Shade-of-Winter’s-Betrayal was able to translate, Bertha’s name and the distillery coming up.  
  
Tip once again approached as stealthily as he could, managing to get right up to the stone without any of them noticing. However, the instant he touched the stone, even with the tip of a stick, they all instantly seemed to notice, immediately moving to try and locate him. This gave us the opportunity to approach, wanting to decipher some of their rune work, curious about the structure. Of course, when we cast our spell, in spite of not touching the monolith at all, it seemed to have the same alarming effect on the Drune, and they rounded back to try and catch us…though not before we were able to confirm the runes very much read: “Animate Dead”.

As fascinating as it would be to study it a bit more, we were aware these Drune were unlikely to be in the sharing or intellectual mood given how vital and hidden they probably wanted this stone to remain, so we quickly retreated while Mogget created a fog cloud to cover our escape. Unfortunately we appeared to be pursued by an invisible assailant, as Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water was speared and sliced at, pinned under something heavy. On the off chance it could be a Dream Snail attracted by all our arcane casting, we sent a Flaming Spirit into the creature, which did damage it, but also very much made it target us instead, a scythe like limb slicing deep into us, far more damaging than even Crewythiant was back in Dreg.  
  
Seeing how injured we were and how ineffective most of our group’s attacks were against this creature. Shade-of-Winter’s-Betrayal summoned a full hunting party from the Winter court, which this creature immediately rounded on, Prince Mallowheart himself even riding amongst them, both the hunting party and the Drune now eagerly engaging in battle, the Fae seemingly keen to take out the Endstone. We didn’t stick around to figure out the finer details, instead fleeing back to the hamlet to lay low, perhaps in one of the shallow graves in the backyards of the zombie residents. It seemed the Drune very much routed the frost elves and had much more powerful magics at their disposal, which, on reflection, made us realise what had been attacking us was a powerful summoning spell.

The entire group managed to escape the carnage and rejoin back at Bertha’s distillery, who was more than willing to put us up for the night for suitable coin, especially since Mogget was still invisible. She seemed oddly disappointed the Drune were not dead given it was thanks to them she was alive…in a sense…, but perhaps she tired of her undead nature? Or maybe there was some other aspect to the Endstone she did not care for.  She actually seemed a keen experimenter, wanting to use some of our inkblood to create a different flavour of whiskey, which we respected. We also felt it was a good time to test our newest spell, creating a Pook Morel in front of everyone, briefly explaining the ways it could help out, but most of us turning in for the night. Interestingly, Shade-of-Winter’s-Betrayal seemed to be going through a sort of crisis of conscience, his perception of the Drune having been rather heavily propagandised.

The following day we continued north east in snowy conditions, still on the ley line, but also feeling a sense of Ennui, to the point where the more fey aligned of us were able to point out a deep magic occurring in the area and time seeming to flow weirdly. It was around this point in time we noticed several pearlescent creatures stampeding away rapidly from what looked like a wall of black flame moving swiftly throughout the whole forest. We all took various approaches to avoid the rapidly radiating flame wall, some of us diving off the path and into a small ditch to submerge ourselves, while Mogget managed to jump onto one of the fleeing creatures, now revealed to be blessed unicorns. Despite his chaotic nature, he managed to hold on from being repelled from them while the rest of us were engulfed by the flames, irrespective of our attempts to avoid them. An immediate psychic affect tried to put us all to sleep, and even after we resisted it seemed everything was grey and muted and soporific, Hollb, Tip and Penny all succumbing to the sleep. Smodri managed to revive and awake Hollb, while Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water just kept slapping Tip, even when it appeared to have no effect…sometimes the actions of others remain a mystery to us.

Through a lot of discussion and examination of the effect, it became clear that what we were witnessing was the dream of a giant Psionic Snail and that the effect was its dream engulfing the wood, hence why the unicorns were fleeing. Assumedly the Snail would fall asleep and everything put to sleep in its dream would become its food. Remembering the moss oracle’s warning about how dangerous said snails were while they were dreaming, we resolved to exit the dream and regroup in the nearby town of Meager’s Reach, making note of where it was to dispatch when it awoke.

Meager’s Reach was a largely brick based town of many colourful glazes and we even witnessed someone with red spiked hair being expelled from the church, which no doubt will attract the attention of Nannigrew if no one else. Hopefully we won’t get too distracted from our hunt, however!

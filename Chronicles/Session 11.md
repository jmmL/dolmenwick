# Chronicler’s notes
Chronicler: Ilick

_Vessel Sam and Vessel Robin moving amongst the rocks along the shores of Fog Lake. Successfully escorted Vessel Gwendolyn to Prigwort. Searching for disappearance of other Vessels. Unaware of time dilation. Discovered smashed musical instruments. Smoke in area indicates narcotic Azoth being developed. Purpose for Vessel Oystus head of Woodcutter Encampment._

We heard a loud voice complaining about the snakes and the terrain and recognised it as Samwise Crumwaller, whom we’d travelled with previously. It seemed most of our group had moved on without us, only Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water remaining, with a letter having been left for Shade-of-Winter’s-Betrayal, presumably from Thinwhistle. We hailed Sam over, who seemed to be accompanied by Robin Tollmein, who we hadn’t seen in some time either. They regaled us with news we had missed out on while we were in the Fairy Roads, most notably that the sisters in the Hall of Harridan got arrested by the Pluritine Church, the crack down on unauthorised magic and witches and what not being the result of a number of libraries and churches being raided by Crookhorns.

Sam was very keen on getting his book of holy figures signed off on by a witness, so we provided no less than five paragraphs as to his exploits and discoveries, while Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water revealed her obsession with St. Nuncy, whom Sam gave more information about as being a minor saint.

Nonetheless it was the second of Haelholm, still in the un-season Chame, and the four of us decided to head to the nearby monastery of the Refuge of St. Key. We feared the journey might take a significant religious bend given Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water’s compulsion and Samwise’s eagerness to peddle his craft, so to speak, but it did mean we were heading towards Orbswallow, which put us in possibly the best position to try and get towards the Myconom. We knew of a lucrative treasure stash nearby so we were hopeful this might convince them to keep accompanying us in this direction.

As Sam explained that St. Keys was the patron saint of chroniclers and lost things, Robin ironically got us lost in a glade of beech and oak, Sam whacking his head as we ended up in front of a cave of with stacked boulders and more notably a large pile of humanoid remains. Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water was convinced this would be a good time to shout out as loud as possible to St. Keys that we had arrived, which prompted a thirty foot brown and black scaled winged serpent to come out, something we would later identify as a Wyrm.

Surprisingly, Sam had a very specific religious ability, we believe it was called St. Dunk’s Plea, to charm serpents, so he did his best to keep the Wyrm pacified, swaying and coiled, while we all fled as fast as we could. Considering the absolute fortuitous convenience of the ability as well as the fact it appeared to work, we believe he underwent something of an actual religious awakening, whereas before we were reasonably certain he was more a practitioner of convenience rather than true faith or belief. It was rather amusing to watch, in all honesty.

Finally back on the Horse Eye road, we reached the Refuge of St. Key, run by Wistan Spatulard, a rather boisterous abbot who pointed out it was the only active Monastery in Dolmenwood, with St. Clewyd’s Abbey being the only other such potential building that could be recovered on their pilgrimage. As Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water cheerfully and wholeheartedly professed their devotion to the Fey Pluritine God, we helpfully added our own interpretation of the Pluritine God being fungal and multifaceted, with Sam very much trying to silence or talk over us both. He needn’t have worried, however, as we connected rather well with the abbot over discussions of beer, mentioning the specialist brewing processes of Orbswallow and Mulchgrove. He commented that the seven ale houses of Prigwort were actually rumoured to be consulting with Prince Mallowheart in order to create their special ale with Fairy help, which evolved an earlier rumour we had heard.

He then also helpfully commented that the best place to get the bones of the defeated witch properly cleansed and disposed of would be the Cathedral of St. Signis near Castle Brackenwold, who specialised in dealing with the undead. He even revealed some useful information about St. Nuncy and a potential relic in Lankshorn which would coincide with reports of relics being stolen or going missing in a similar area? We tried a few of their ales as well before resting in the monastery for the night, and predictably for St. Keys, the prayer Sam was able to obtain was Locate Object.

_Vessel Dapple dreams. Statue of St. Nuncy. Witch Owls. Dark Cave…_  
  
It being the fourth of Chame, we continued on the Horse Eye road, heading in the direction of Orbswallow at our insistence, even convincing Robin to continue to accompany us for another day, despite her duty wanting her to head back to Prigwort. We passed a crossroads which pointed north towards “The Bakery”, and as we continued to explore we noticed a curious sensation amongst elm and hawthorn trees, putting together that we must be locating the area of another ley line, although this emotion was different. There was an acrid, acidic smell, and Robin pointed out a curious monolith resting in a bog like area nearby. Clearly magical, we decided to investigate.

The monolith appeared to be both solid and made of light and smoke, and Robin seemed to be affected by it as we studied it, suddenly convinced she was the favoured Scion of the Earl of Yellow and needed to return to Prigwort so she could join up with the hooded figures that patrolled its perimeter. Sam was having none of it and decided to deface the monolith with a symbol of the Pluritine Church, but nothing appeared to happen.

We pressed on to Orbswallow and did our best to introduce our companions to our culture and structures, finding the reversal of the usual situation we are often in very amusing as we pointed out the mycotecture, the Moon Tree, Milk Tree and the Pipe Tree. Sam’s discomfort in particular was delightful, especially when we explained in great scientific detail the prevalence of fungi and the makeup of all living and dead material as consisting of many fungal and microscopic organisms. At this point we think his brain broke and he retreated into the comfort of thinking this was all a “Mossling Religion” and took him and Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water into the Sombrelamb for some refreshments.

They were somewhat nonplussed by the Glubwub, but seemed to tolerate the Moon’s Milk, and we of course were partial to the New Moon Soup which stained everything black, much like our cap. We all managed to pick up some very interesting rumours, one about ale, one about a woodgrue compost heap, and one about Nutcap raiders trying to brew a special kind of poison just for Mosslings! We also found out that odd monolith only appears in the summer months. All in all it was a pleasant end to the day.

The following morning we wrote a letter for Robin to hand off as she set off towards Prigwort so that she wouldn’t just immediately get into trouble and that potentially someone there could relieve her condition, probably Mosslemyre Drouge. We also bought several pipes for Smodri, though we suspect being another native to this area, he probably was aware of where to get them. Then we arrived at the white mushroom shrine just at the eastern edge of town.

While we were sorely tempted to eat the offerings and commune with the fungi growing there, we knew we were very close to where the Myconom was reported to be and we did not want to waste any more time in our efforts to get there, knowing just how long a communion with fungi could potentially take. Sam was obviously not going to partake either given his aversion to pretty much everything here.

Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water, however, had no such qualms and consumed a creamy cheese offering that turned her purple and green, caused her to collapse, and we lost her for approximately seven hours.

_Vessel Dapple. Statue of St. Nuncy. Witch Owls. Realms of Absinthe Lakes. Mansion of Red Shadows. Consuming, Eating Prince Who is Seven. Ochre skinned satyr with mane of gold. Told to “Seek the place Breggles are wed. Seek the Troth Stone.”_

Once Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water had recovered we continued down a black road which led us through of cluster of sycamore trees where we saw some climbing fungus being used as a kind of passage to a tree supported structure. It turned out to be the dwelling of a Nutcap researcher, who was eager to greet us, offering calming sap wine and explaining he’d been trying to create an Elixir of Life by crossbreeding plants and using the materials pilfered from Orbswallow. He had tried to obtain consent and cooperations of the Mosslings, but Brimble the chief Mulcher was not happy with the tests or results, which soon became apparent as to why when it seemed the old Nutcap’s efforts merely turned the subjects into plants and trees, though the Nutcap failed to see why this was an issue. It even brought up a rather amusing theological discussion over the nature of Hell and sinning from the other two…

# Chronicler’s notes
Chronicler: Ilick

We lost track of Shade-of-Winter’s-Betrayal as well as Hollb Puffball, the former presumably laying low after provoking that skirmish around the End Stone whilst the latter likely became distracted with a more down to earth matter. As we wandered the winding lanes of the town, we noticed a rather curious discrepancy in the familial tombs of Everslumber Lane…no one seems to have been interred at all for one hundred and fifty years in the period between two hundred and fifty to a hundred years ago! Perhaps they were sensibly composting their dead for a while?

It turned out in a book Tip discovered at a tower in the library that Dream Snails tend to feed on and around ley lines, which is potentially useful for our continued hunt. Moggét consumed some of the mushroom from the shrine in Orbswallow, thankfully not seeming to have quite as intense or long a reaction as Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water did, but apparently succumbed to a dream involving a river, a village, a hawk, a rat, and several hanging crookhorns.

The next day, on the 5th of Reedwyrm, Smodri decided to perform a ballad of the battle we witnessed between the Drune and the Elves by the Endstone, attracting some more rumours [4 & 13]. As he did so we all noticed a retinue of eight elven nights accompanying a rather notable woman with black hair, patrician beauty, and a diamond tiara, notably talking about said battle. It transpired that this was none other than the sorceress Ygraine, and she was very keen to learn more about what had transpired. Smodri and the others, of course, wanted to know what we could get out of her before we told her anything, so she offered a number of tempting prospects, including using Draxus Jerricorn’s tools to make us any sort of magical item we would want, as well as the pick of a grove of magical apples that could upgrade weapons in an arcane manner. 

She also assured us that Mosselmyre Druge was the only arcanist who could identify things, having somewhat cornered all the research on it, which of course made us wish to perhaps pursue an avenue towards it to cut him out…but that could come after our entreaties to the Myconom.

When questioned about her associations to the rumours about dream and her involvement, she grew rather reticent, and instead seemed to want to focus more on what we knew, believing the attack on the Endstone was the work of Duke Myfleur, none of us denying the assumption, not wanting to get Shade-of-Winter’s-Betrayal in trouble, after all. She then mentioned something which…would become somewhat of an obstacle in our particular path…speaking of how Giant Snails were often ridden by goblins and could be used as mounts and taught…which immediately enraptured both Tip and Moggét, the two of them losing sight entirely of our objective and now just wanting to ride the snails. She also put forward that only high level Fairy magic would be able to do anything for Bill the plant now, though she already knew of Creekeek and his experiments.

As we travelled towards the site of the battle with her and her retinue, she eventually admitted that the entire town of Meager’s Reach had been magically brought back by her from utter extinction, wiped out by some sort of plague or disease and that the town existed in a kind of dreamlike state where any inhabitant who tried to venture too far from its boundaries would vanish. This would of course explain the discrepancy in the tomb dates and also the discord between the elders and the rest of the town, as it seemed like a very tricky situation of freedom vs protection and an example of when the arcane arts can perhaps go a bit too far…we’ve never really understood the preoccupation with staving off death, when it is such an essential part of life, but we understand it is a motivator for many.

Around the area of the Endstone there now appeared to be animated bramble guards summoned by the Drune, obviously not taking any chances. Igraine and her party seemed to wish to contact Lady Harrowmore and agonising over which ArchDrune to contact and figure out a way to solve the situation diplomatically. The sorceress seemed to have something of a peace keeping role or perhaps compulsion. 

Either way, we decided it was better to continue on our own quest and left her, travelling through the woods to come upon an area of fly infested bogs, moss beards and sandstone crags, in particular spotting an eighty foot tall sandstone protrusion with a waterfall pouring into a pool. There was the sound of pipe music and singing and an orange creature with a large spiral shell appeared to be washing itself. This, of course, was a Dream Snail and we grew excited to finally have found what we’d been searching for over the last month or so, but first Tip snuck closer to see if anything else was going on, given there appeared to be others present. He found a bunch of woodgrue in a cave behind the waterfall with barrels of very sweet mead, seeming to be enjoying themselves with no clue as to the Giant Snail lingering outside.

Then…?  
  
_Vessel Moggét used potent fairy enchantment…ruined OUR plan for Vessel Ilick…made the offering his own companion…tore us into different time…we shall not forget this, oh no, we shall not, Vessel Moggét, your death shall be painful, humiliating and entirely your own fault, yes…  
  
_…things seemed a bit fuzzy, but we decided to explore behind the waterfall once Tip told us it was safe and Moggét tended to his mount and brother Conchwin the Giant Dream Snail. We did of course feel it was perhaps awkward to be searching for another of his kin to offer to the Myconom, Moggét seemed to think we would carve up Conchwin when he was not looking…but we’re not the sort to harvest another’s valued partner. Besides, Conchwin was a very profitable bed for growing all sorts of fungi! We have always had a very great relationship and we’re not sure where Moggét’s paranoia seemed to be coming from.

We became interested in the violet moss growing in the cave, as well as the dew berries, while Smodri approached one of the Woodgrue wearing a crown who appeared to be in charge, proclaiming himself to be the King of all Woodgrue, though this fact was extremely debatable. Smodri offered to get others to come join the celebration and acknowledge him for various boons, and then we all proceeded to explore outwards further.

We came upon some sort of statue buried beneath an oak tree, which Nannigrew was keen to excavate, all of us helping to expose and discover a lost shrine of St Whittery, who appeared to be the saint tied to visions and premonitions and wards against Fairy. We camped for the night, finding some lank leaf root and then also witnessing a scene of four dire wolves teaching a small dire wolf pup how to howl in a nearby clearing.

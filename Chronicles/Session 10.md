# Chronicler’s notes
Chronicler: Ilick

As we descended downstairs to use the large candle in the workshop, we discovered Tip, Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water and Bogfrink gathered around one of the painted portals, trying to figure out how to activate it. They were apparently successful on their mission, having had to deliver some chocolates to prove their worth to Lady Shantywood, and also Tip went on something called a booze cruise? They also discovered that the river was being polluted near Hag’s Addle.

_Or at least, that is what we observed the Vessels achieving. We are at this moment uncertain as to their larger reaching effects._

As we showed them how the rune etched candles worked, Tip grew distracted by the butterflies within the workshop and it became swiftly apparent that Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water was now somewhat obsessed with St. Nuncy thanks to the armour she was wearing. We shall have to make sure not to carelessly use anything that used to belong to a witch or any other dubious user of the arcane arts, though we imagine many would have a similar opinion about our own practice.

Curiously the large candle seemed to not only make the large door manifest, but also every other door in the workshop. Perhaps it was used for greater ease of passage? The large portal opened up into an inky black doorway that seemed to have a dark forest on the other side, but before we ventured in Tip noticed an odd, out of place shadow revealing a hidden weapon, some form of magical runed short sword, propped unobtrusively against the doorframe.

As we passed through the portal we could hear furious bleating and saw shadowy tendrils moving through the air and land. Everything also appeared to grow more and more monochromatic, including ourselves, and we finally came upon Odelle Oakenbleat in a desperate fight against two of those vengeful shadow spirits draining his strength we had observed in the dining room back in the castle. Perhaps they had escaped from this realm the last time the door was opened?

Tip slew one of the spirits with his new blade while Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water managed to distract the other with her Awe ability. This allowed Nanigrew to grab Odelle and get him to safety while Shade-of-Winter’s-Betrayal dispatched the other spirit fighting back to back. Observing the land, Smodri took the time to do his wood kenning on the local trees, which only had Odelle’s name along with various unrecognisable Elven names. The moon also appeared quite different.

We had a sneaking suspicion which was almost immediately confirmed when we all attempted to return through the portal to the workshop in Candlestone Fortress: as we approached, the portal vanished! Though we should have had at least a few hours before the candle burned down completely, it seemed time flowed differently here, and we were very much in some sort of Fairy realm. The two elves in the party confirmed that this appeared to be the Fairy Roads, which were there to navigate through Fairy, reaching other realms through various portals, though one needed an invitation to enter anywhere they didn’t come from. Tip scaled a tree to see the white gravel path and where it might lead, and apparently the logic was usually to follow the path, but even the choice to not follow the path could sometimes be a path? A bit vague, but we suppose we can’t really comment.

As it was late for all of us we decided to make camp within the Fairy Roads, Nannigrew making food while Tip and Shade-of-Winter’s-Betrayal made music. Smodri and Bogfrink came up with the lyrics to the music, the subject being a “Mossling named Bumble”. A decent enough evening to be sure, but we were woken up in the middle of the night by other music being played, Smodri quickly countering it with his own playing, as it had some sort of charming or soporific effect.

The music was coming from three humanoid looking women, one extremely pale with a shrivelled head around her neck. They appeared to be digging in the ground with daggers in an almost ceremonial or rhythmic way, and the language they were singing appeared to be some form of Drunic. It transpired they were harvesting mandrakes, hence the reason for the enchanting melody. With some talking on behalf of the others, we were able to gain a lot of deep rooted information from them, from the tale of the first banishment of the Elves from Dolmenwood, the Ring of Chell ley line, and thirteen lunar cycles being important to the witches we had discovered…but of course, the most important thing they told of was the location of the brain of a sentient fungal god by the name of the Myconom.

Privately we resolved to ensure we would find this Myconom as soon as possible, but first, we had to leave the Fairy Roads and end up back in the wood. The three Drune mandrake harvesters felt it was best to head towards the Mizzle Door which would open up near the Fog Lake and a Drune settlement and so we accompanied them, heading along the gravel path. We first encountered a kind of malignant phantom bound to an oozing liquid along the path, which was dealt with fairly easily given it couldn’t move from its spot. Then, further down the path, we came across an orchard full of crystalline apples which were labelled to belong to “The Regent Hadon”.

Tip, of course, could not resist immediately climbing the tree and swiping one of them, to which the three Drune women immediately objected, as apparently anyone in possession of those apples would be incapable of singing…which we understand would be rather devastating for them given that was how they worked their own magics. We suspect Smodri rather cannily wanted to get the apple planted on them at some point.

The next occurrence along the path was a rather suspicious pile of two naked humanoids who claimed they were robbed by Red Caps, but kept trying to induce us off the path into the brush, feeling very much like an ambush. Their breath smelled weirdly blossom like and the Drune very much thought something was wrong, Dapple-of-Moon-on-Water immediately noticed they were shape shifters, in spite of giving human names that seemed to be true enough from Smodri’s wood kenning.

They turned out to be rather vicious ooze creatures that try to steal the forms and identities of others, so we all turned on them, managing to completely freeze one and melt the other with a combination of our spell casting and the abilities of the others. We then discovered a rather sizeable treasure hoard, presumably from other adventurers and travellers they had consumed, which included a rather magnificent magical staff made of pesilian and rune carved into a wood like motif. No doubt a powerful magical artefact and focus, though we suspect Bogfrink might get more use from it and feel more at home using it than we, taking a more unorthodox approach to our arcane magics.

The Drune took the opportunity to run ahead of us to the portal so that the time dilation would prevent us from knowing which way they went and thus keep the location of their settlement a secret, but when we did finally emerge out the Mizzle Door we realised we were in the Un-season of Chaim, the sky having turned purple and an overabundance of snakes coming out everywhere. As we sought out camp, a frost elf by the name of Thinwhistle, whom some of the party had previously encountered and freed, came to greet us.

He seemed singularly focused on wanting to know where the Mizzle Door was and the pathway through the Fairy Roads, but everyone was sensibly quite tight lipped. He provided a lot of interesting rumours and was keen to talk of an alliance between the Lady of Midnight and Winter happening near Lake Vernal. Obviously had some form of bad blood with the Drune given his disparaging mention of the Ring of Chell, we found it curious too that he was very pointedly acting like he didn’t know Shade-of-Winter’s-Betrayal, but Fairy politics have never really interested us in particular. Perhaps the others may get more use of the information, we will settle down for the night.
